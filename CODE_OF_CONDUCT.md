# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or the language they are most comfortable in. Questions asked in Arabic, English or anything else are equally welcome.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assuming good intent and asking before concluding.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled with discretion.
